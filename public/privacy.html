## Privacy Policy

VERIFIED POLICY
Publisher: Andy Davis (Andy's Dev Studio)

1. Introduction & Studio Philosophy

I, Andy Davis, operate Andy's Dev Studio as an independent development foundry and personal portfolio. I respect your privacy unconditionally. My applications—including Infinite Drafting, Factor Hunter, and web prototypes—are engineered for mathematical utility, creative drafting, and offline performance. I have no interest in collecting, tracking, monetizing, or selling your personal data.

2. Information Collection & Local Storage

  • No Analytics or Ad Tracking: Our applications do not incorporate third-party advertising SDKs, tracking pixels, or invasive behavioral profiling.
  • Local-Only Data: Any project files, drafting states, custom settings, or progress created within Infinite Drafting or other studio apps are stored strictly locally on your device (via internal app storage or browser localStorage). We do not operate remote servers that harvest your drafts or calculations.
  • Export & File Access: When you export drawings, images, or save files in Infinite Drafting, access is requested solely to write and save the requested files to your designated storage directory.

3. Communications

If you voluntarily reach out via email (andys.dev.studio@gmail.com), Discord, or the Raven contact form on Andy's Dev Studio, your email address and message content will only be used to respond directly to your inquiry or bug report. Your contact details will never be sold, leased, rented, or distributed to any third party.

4. Children's Online Privacy Protection

Our applications do not knowingly collect any personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13. Since no personal data is collected or transmitted from any user, our tools are safe for students, educators, and general audiences.

5. Security & Changes

Because we do not store your personal information on external servers, the security of your created work resides safely with your personal device. If this policy is ever updated, revisions will be published immediately on this page and reflected in the Guild Hall records at Andy's Dev Studio.
