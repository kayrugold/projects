1. Print-on-Demand Production

All physical studio provisions (shirts, hoodies, mugs, and prints) are custom made to order. Because items are printed fresh upon order confirmation, please allow a creation and print production window of 2 to 5 business days prior to carrier dispatch.

2. Transit Times & Delivery

  • Domestic Shipping (US): Standard delivery typically takes 2 to 5 business days following production.
  • International Shipping: Transit durations range between 10 to 30 business days depending on destination customs clearance.
  • Carrier Tracking: Tracking numbers are automatically emailed upon package dispatch so you can monitor your freight in transit.

3. Shipping Rates & Calculation

Accurate shipping costs are dynamically calculated at checkout directly through our Printify integration based on item weight, dimensions, and your designated shipping address. Any applicable regional sales taxes or import duties are itemized transparently prior to payment.

4. Damaged or Lost Shipments

If your provisions arrive damaged during transit or fail to arrive within the designated carrier window, contact me immediately with your order number and photos of the damage. I will arrange an immediate replacement or full refund without hassle.
